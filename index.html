Praana — Behavioral Awareness Platform

Praana is an AI-powered behavioral awareness platform designed to help you build a healthier relationship with your digital devices through mindfulness, focus rooms, and Saathi, your digital wellness coach. Your conscious layer between impulse and action.

Features

  • Focus Sessions: Timed deep work with a minimal timer, calming prompts, and zero distractions.
  • Breathing Overlay: Mindful transition prompts before opening a distracting app.
  • Focus Rooms: Co-work in silence with others in a shared canopy of focus.
  • Saathi AI: A conscious AI companion and reflective space for journaling.
  • App Protection: Wrap your most triggering apps in a conscious layer.
