Legal Documents — Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Praana is a behavioral awareness platform designed to help you build a healthier relationship with your digital devices. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create a Praana account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name (optional)
  • Authentication data via Firebase Authentication (Google sign-in or email/password)

1.2 Behavioral & Usage Data

To provide personalized awareness insights, Praana collects:

  • Screen time patterns and app usage frequency (read from Android UsageStats API with your permission)
  • Number of phone pickups and unlock events
  • Focus session data — duration, interruptions, completion status
  • Apps you choose to protect during focus sessions
  • Overlay interaction data — which stage you reached, what you chose
  • Onboarding questionnaire responses (emotional patterns, focus goals, vulnerable timings)

1.3 Saathi Conversation Data

When you use Saathi (our AI companion), we collect:

  • Messages you send and Saathi's responses
  • Emotion tags derived from conversation content (e.g., "stressed", "anxious")
  • Behavioral pattern tags derived from usage context
  • Long-term behavioral summaries generated from conversation history

1.4 Focus Room Data

  • Rooms you create or join
  • Participation duration and focus status
  • Room energy state interactions

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Provide personalized behavioral awareness insights
  • Power Saathi's emotionally intelligent responses using your behavioral context and memory
  • Generate your daily insights, weekly reflections, and 21-day behavioral plans
  • Detect vulnerable usage patterns (nighttime scrolling, stress-driven app opening)
  • Improve the relevance of interruption overlays and story reflections
  • Maintain your focus session history and progress

⚠️ We do NOT use your data for advertising, we do NOT sell your data to third parties, and we do NOT use your data to maximize your screen time or engagement with Praana.

3. AI Processing (Saathi)

Saathi is powered by Groq's Llama AI model. When you send a message to Saathi:

  • Your message and behavioral context are sent to Groq's API for processing
  • Groq's privacy policy applies to this processing — see groq.com/privacy-policy
  • We do not store your raw messages on Groq's servers — only the final response is retained in our Firebase database
  • Emotion and behavior tags are generated locally and by our own Node.js backend before reaching any AI model

4. Data Storage & Security

  • All user data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore (praana-b3b61)
  • Data is encrypted in transit using TLS/HTTPS
  • Firestore security rules ensure users can only access their own data
  • Your Saathi conversation history is stored under your unique user ID, accessible only to you
  • We do not store API keys or sensitive credentials in the app code

5. Permissions We Request

Praana requests the following Android permissions:

  • Usage Access (UsageStatsManager): To read app usage patterns for behavioral insights. We only read aggregate statistics — not the content of other apps.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): To show the interruption overlay when you open a protected app during focus mode.
  • Accessibility Service: To detect when a protected app is opened during a focus session, enabling the overlay to appear. We do not read screen content, passwords, or personal information from other apps.
  • Foreground Service: To keep the focus session timer running when the app is in the background.
  • Internet: To sync data with Firebase and communicate with the Saathi AI backend.
  • Notifications: To send focus reminders and daily behavioral insights.

6. Data Retention

  • Conversation history: retained for 30 days for behavioral memory, then summarized and raw messages deleted
  • Focus session data: retained indefinitely for personal progress tracking
  • Account data: retained until you delete your account
  • You can request deletion of all your data at any time by contacting us

7. Children's Privacy

Praana is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your conversation history and behavioral data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

9. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of significant changes to this privacy policy via the app or email. Continued use of Praana after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
